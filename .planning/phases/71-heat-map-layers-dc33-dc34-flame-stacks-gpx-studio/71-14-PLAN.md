<objective>
Close the scheduler-side warnings that make a slow or unlucky build dangerous during the con.

**WR-04:** the hourly expression fires at the top of every hour across 5-10 August 2026 and
the daily baseline fires at 04:00, both in Pacific time. On each of the six con days they
coincide exactly. EventBridge invokes the Lambda twice in the same minute, there is no
reserved concurrency on the function, no idempotency key and no lock in the builder, so two
full DynamoDB scans and two full S3 fan-outs run concurrently and both write the same key.
The module header names overlapping builds as the landmine to avoid, and then the live unit
schedules one deterministically.

**WR-03 (infrastructure half):** `lambda_timeout` is set EQUAL to a number that turns out to
be fictional anyway. Equal is not enough even taken at face value — the Lambda's budget must
also absorb the SSM round trip, cold start, DNS and connection setup — so a genuinely slow
build kills the invoker before the response arrives, the invoker throws, and the retry
policy fires up to two more invocations, each starting a fresh full rebuild while the first
is still scanning.

Also closes WR-08 (no confused-deputy condition on either trust policy, notably the
EventBridge Scheduler one AWS explicitly documents as needing it) and WR-09's invoker half.

Purpose: plan 71-11 gave the builder a real 240 s deadline. This plan makes the two outer
bounds agree with it and removes the one guaranteed collision on the calendar.

Output: schedules that cannot coincide, a strictly increasing timeout chain, and trust
policies that name their expected caller's account.
</objective>
