<objective>
Twenty of the 110 features in the live DC33 artifact are entirely `[[0,0],[0,0]]` — zero-length
lines at null island, produced when a short or degenerate DC33 summary polyline decodes to
zero accumulated deltas. They draw nothing, they contribute nothing to `totalKm`, and they
inflate the publicly-served `runCount` by 22%: the studio's HEAT MAP section says "110 runs"
when about 90 ran. The standalone verifier certified the artifact anyway, because its range
check verifies bounds and not degeneracy.

Plan 71-10 added the filter at the assembly point and taught the verifier to catch it. This
plan is the operational half: re-run the frozen DC33 backfill so the fix reaches the object
production actually serves.

**This changes a number the public sees.** It is a correction, not a regression, but it is
visible — which is why it is gated on a human rather than published silently.

Purpose: the artifact is frozen and will never be rebuilt on a schedule. If it is not
rebuilt here, the wrong count ships through the con and the fix in 71-10 protects only DC34.

Output: a republished DC33 artifact with no degenerate features, an updated machine-readable
contract line, and an invalidated CDN entry.
</objective>
