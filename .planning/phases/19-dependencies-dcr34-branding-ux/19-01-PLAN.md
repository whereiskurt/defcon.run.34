## tlora-t3s3 quirk background
`apps/run.flash/webapp/src/hooks/use-flash.ts` currently calls `espLoader.writeFlash({ flashSize: "keep", flashMode: "keep", flashFreq: "keep", ... })` at lines 97–117. `"keep"` tells esptool-js to preserve whatever flash parameters are already encoded in the firmware image's header. For most ESP32 devices this is correct. For `tlora-t3s3`, upstream Meshtastic/flasher.meshtastic.org history documents that an explicit `flashMode: 'dio'` is required or the device fails to boot after flash. ROADMAP explicitly requires this quirk to be carried across the dep bump.

The device identifier to branch on is `device.platformioTarget` (already used by `getFactoryFilename` in `src/config/firmware.ts` — same field, same string, no lookup needed).
