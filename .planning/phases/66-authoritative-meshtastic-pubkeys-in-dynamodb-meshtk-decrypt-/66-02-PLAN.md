<objective>
Wire the authoritative write: the internal `register-radio` endpoint that run.flash calls must, in addition to its existing behavior, upsert the `MeshRadio` entity — converting the device's base64 X25519 pubkey to `0x` hex once at this boundary and canonicalizing `nodeId` (pad-8 lowercase) with an explicit `nodeNum`. Factor the pure conversions into a standalone lib module so the backfill (plan 66-04) and any future writer reuse identical logic (L2/L3/L10).

Purpose: This is the single point where a device's real on-device pubkey becomes the DDB-authoritative decrypt key meshtk reads. Getting the encoding and canonicalization right here is Success Criterion #1's foundation.
Output: `mesh-radio-canonical.ts` (+ test) and a MeshRadio upsert wired into the internal register route.
</objective>
