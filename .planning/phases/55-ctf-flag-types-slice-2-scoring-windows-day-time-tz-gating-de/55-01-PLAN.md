    - DEFCON_RUN_HOURS resolves to days {0,4,5,6} (Sun,Thu,Fri,Sat), from "06:00", to "08:00", tz "America/Los_Angeles".
    - Fail-closed: an invalid/undecodable tz (Intl throws) returns false (deny), never a leak of the reason — documented as the safe default.
