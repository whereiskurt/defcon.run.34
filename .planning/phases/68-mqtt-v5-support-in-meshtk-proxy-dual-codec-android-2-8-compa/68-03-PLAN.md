<objective>
Prove the dual codec against a real broker, then land it upstream. A live mosquitto behind the
proxy with a v5 client and a 3.1.1 client in the SAME run is the only test that exercises the
parts unit tests cannot reach: the actual byte stream through `StartProxyServer`, mosquitto's
own view of the swapped identity, real CONNACK properties from the broker, PUBACK round trips,
and graceful DISCONNECT handling.

Then the upstream PR is opened and merged. That merge is the hand-off to plan 68-04: the
monorepo's CI build clones `whereiskurt/meshtk` main at image-build time, so the dependency pin
and the vendored codec only reach production through upstream main.

Purpose: MQV5-06 in full, plus the upstream half of MQV5-07.
Output: a gated end-to-end test with its mosquitto fixture, a green full suite, and a merged
upstream PR.
</objective>
