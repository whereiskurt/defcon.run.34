<objective>
Ship the dual codec to production, prove it on the wire, then hand off to human UAT.

The deploy runs in GitHub Actions — never a local terragrunt apply. Local tooling only builds
and pushes images; `deploy.yml` applies ECS. Everything up to and including the prod wire check
must be machine-verified; the last step is Kurt with the Android 2.8.0-open.6 APK, which is the
only way to confirm mqttastic itself is satisfied.

The decisive prod signal is small and unambiguous: before this phase, a v5 CONNECT to
mqtt.defcon.run:4433 gets `2003008400` (0x84 Unsupported Protocol Version) from the v0.0.70
honest-reject preflight. After the deploy, the same CONNECT with deliberately wrong credentials
must get `2003008700` (0x87 Not authorized) — that byte flip can only happen if the v5 codec is
parsing the CONNECT and running it through the Authenticator.

Purpose: the ship-and-verify half of MQV5-07, and the evidence base for ROADMAP success criteria
1, 2 and 4.
Output: a deployed image, a captured before/after wire trace, log evidence for both protocols,
and a human UAT checkpoint.
</objective>

  <what-built>
    The meshtk proxy at mqtt.defcon.run:4433 now speaks MQTT 5.0 alongside 3.1.1, chosen
    per-connection at the CONNECT preflight. On a v5 connection: credentials are verified through
    the same DynamoDB-backed Authenticator and swapped to the mosquitto proxy identity before the
    broker sees anything; PUBLISH packets are decoded, judged by the same rules engine, hop-clamped
    and payload-rewritten onto the v5 wire; downlink is logged and self-echoes suppressed; topic
    aliases are suppressed in both directions. Bad credentials answer 0x87, enhanced auth answers
    0x8C, and 0x84 remains only for protocol levels above 5. The 3.1.1 path is unchanged.

    Machine-verified before reaching you: full upstream unit suite including a 3.1.1 byte-identity

    traffic across the deploy.
