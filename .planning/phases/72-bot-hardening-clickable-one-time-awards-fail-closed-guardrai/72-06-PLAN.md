Replace ricky's literal QR-path finale with two reliable award DMs carrying a short
single-use claim link, and promote line 01 to the reliable send path.

BLE), not to a QoS0 reconnect gap — so a reliable retry is the right fix there. The
guard test that pins the reliable-site count moves from exactly-1 to exactly-3. That is
a DELIBERATE change, not an accident: the lyric body must stay single-shot.

Purpose: the award becomes tappable and genuinely one-time, and the two messages that
actually matter stop getting dropped.
Output: a widened lyric session record, a mint-by-challenge client, the award sends, and
an updated guard test.
