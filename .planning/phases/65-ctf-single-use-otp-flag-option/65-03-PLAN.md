<objective>
Surface the single-use option in the admin CTF form (CTFT-18) and carry it through the write + redaction seams (the admin half of CTFT-15): a `singleUse` toggle (default off) in the Rotating-OTP section, posted as `otp.singleUse` and persisted verbatim by the existing OTP passthrough, and preserved through secret-redaction so the edit form rehydrates it. No judge or entity logic here — this is the operator-facing control plane that feeds the flag the judge (65-02) enforces.

Purpose: Give operators a per-flag switch to make a rotating-OTP flag first-come single-use, completing the config surface (CTFT-15) at the admin layer and delivering CTFT-18.
Output: `singleUse` on the `InputCtf.otp` / `LoadedCtfRecord.otp` / `RedactedCtfRecord.otp` types; `redactCtfSecrets` preserving it; a toggle in `CtfForm.tsx` Section 3b wired to the posted otp payload; passthrough + redaction unit tests.
</objective>
